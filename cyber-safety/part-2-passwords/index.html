PART 2 · KEEP IT PRIVATE

A strong password still
fails if it leaks.

Length and complexity cannot protect a password that other people can see.

▧Photo or screenshot

A password appears in the background.

●Group chat or message

☺Personal information

Names, birthdays, pets and teams can be public clues.

↻The same password
