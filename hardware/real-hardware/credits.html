Hardware models & credits

Detailed 3D representations of computer hardware, used to recognise physical parts. Product names identify representative examples, not purchase recommendations. The models are not a validated set of compatible parts.

Models are hosted with the lesson website. Display orientation, lighting and scale are adjusted. The server cabinet uses replacement metal and glass materials because the source DAE download omits its texture files. Selected files are optimised with glTF Transform (mesh compression and resized WebP textures); the preview images are renders of these same models. They are not photographs or scans of our classroom equipment.

Sketchfab files were obtained from the attributed redistributions in CustomPc and pc-anatomy, with licences verified against Sketchfab's public model API on 11 October 2026. Innerscene files were downloaded from their original CC0 library pages. No BuildCores assets are used.

Classroom detail adaptations: the credited CPU mesh is shown twice with added Intel/Core and AMD/Ryzen AM4 labels, representative LGA contact pads and PGA pins. These are illustrative adaptations, not exact manufacturer CAD; AMD AM5 also uses LGA. The Corsair RAM mesh is shown with its heat spreader hidden, replacement PCB/chip materials, gold edge contacts, a generic label and board traces. The SSD has added SATA power/data contact geometry. Contacts are simplified for visibility rather than exact pin-count engineering diagrams.
